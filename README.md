# HR-analytics-dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** developed using Power BI to analyze employee data and identify key workforce trends. The dashboard provides insights into employee attrition, demographics, salary distribution, job roles, education, and years of service.

### Key Features
- **Employee Overview:** Total employees, attrition count, attrition rate, average age, average salary, and average years of service.
- **Attrition Analysis:** Analyzes employee attrition based on gender, age groups, and education.
- **Salary Analysis:** Tracks employee attrition across different salary slabs.
- **Job Role Analysis:** Identifies attrition patterns across various job roles.
- **Experience Analysis:** Examines attrition based on years spent at the company.
- **Department Insights:** Includes Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales.

### Key Metrics
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employee Attrition: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Average Age: 37 Years
- Average Salary: 6.5K
- Average Years at Company: 7 Years

### Objective
To transform employee data into meaningful visual insights, understand workforce attrition patterns, and support better HR decision-making.
